WHAT WE DO

Preserving your family memories.

Memory Factory offers a plethora of media services. We can digitize your older video formats such as VHS, Hi8 and VHSC tapes. We can even handle more professional grade formats such as BetacamSP, HDCAM, DVCPRO and pretty much any other tape and disc format you may have lying around.

We also handle image scanning, from negatives to prints. We scan at a high resolution on flatbed scanners, so your photos are protected from damaging photo feeders. Our scanning includes basic touch ups as well, we can clean dust, scratches and enhance color too.

Besides scanning, we also can print your images on high quality photo and/or fine art paper. Printing sizes range from wallet size to large format 44″ poster prints.

Memory Factory can transfer old LP’s as well as cassette tapes to digital mp3 files and CD’s. Trust us when we say, when it comes to media transfers, we can do it!
